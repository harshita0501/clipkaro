FAQ

Is ClipKaro free?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser, so there are no server costs.

How fast is it?

Exporting uses your computer's hardware video encoder, typically 2–3× faster than the video's length on a normal laptop (more on a strong GPU). Subtitles run on your graphics chip in Chrome/Edge; without one they fall back to the CPU, which is slower.

Can I clip many videos at once?

Yes. Add as many as you want and press Start. Pick an output folder and every video's clips land in their own subfolder, with an .srt file.

Which languages work?

Hindi, English and 90+ others. Choose the language for best results, and use “Best” accuracy for Hindi. You can fix any caption and re-export just the clips you changed.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Transcription and video rendering happen on your device.

How does Custom Trim work?

Switch to the Custom Trim tab, upload one long video, and type the start and end of each moment (like 33:07 to 34:14). ClipKaro transcribes only those moments, lets you fix the captions, adds your hook text at the top, and exports each one as a 1080×1920 MP4 named like clip_33-07_34-14.mp4.

What format are the clips?

MP4 (H.264 + AAC), 1080×1920 for reels. Uploads straight to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook.
